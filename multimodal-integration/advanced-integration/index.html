Advanced integration

🧠 Key takeaways

Unpaired, reference and mosaic integration combine modalities that were not measured in the same cells, and GLUE is the most accurate method for unpaired RNA and ATAC data.

Multimodal references let unimodal queries borrow the missing modality, but imputed values are only reliable for well-measured features.

MultiVI integrates datasets that share only some modalities, and results should be checked against held-out measurements.

Motivation¶

Modalities are often not measured in the same cells. An RNA atlas may need to be combined with chromatin accessibility from other cells, new data may need to be mapped onto an existing multimodal reference, or datasets may share only some of their modalities. These tasks are called unpaired (diagonal) integration, reference mapping and mosaic integration.

In independent benchmarks, GLUE was the most accurate method for unpaired RNA and ATAC data, MultiVI performed well for mosaic integration of RNA and ATAC, and totalVI predicted proteins from RNA best Hu et al., 2024Fu et al., 2025Liu et al., 2025. We use the bone marrow data of the previous chapter and hold out one modality or part of the data, so that each result can be checked against the measurements.

Unpaired integration with GLUE¶

Without paired cells, RNA and ATAC share no features, so a method needs prior knowledge to connect them. GLUE links each gene to the peaks near its promoter and gene body in a guidance graph and learns embeddings of the cells of both modalities that respect these links Cao & Gao, 2022. We take the RNA of two Multiome donors and the ATAC of the third donor, so that no cell is measured in both, and use the measured labels only to check the result.

A common use of unpaired integration is to annotate ATAC data with an annotated RNA reference. We transfer the labels of the RNA cells to their nearest neighbors among the ATAC cells and compare them with the labels of the ATAC cells.

labels = {}
for level in ("cell_type", "cell_type_l1"):
    knn = KNeighborsClassifier(n_neighbors=15).fit(rna.obsm["X_glue"], rna.obs[level])
    labels[level] = knn.predict(atac.obsm["X_glue"]) == atac.obs[level].to_numpy()
pd.Series({level: correct.mean() for level, correct in labels.items()}, name="accuracy")
cell_type 0.644309 cell_type_l1 0.824959 Name: accuracy, dtype: float64

82% of the ATAC cells receive the correct lineage and 64% the correct fine cell type. Most errors occur between closely related T cell states and in rare progenitors, whose differences are small in chromatin accessibility.

Reference mapping with totalVI¶

A multimodal reference lets us map new data, even of a single modality, onto its embedding and predict the missing modality. totalVI maps a query onto a trained reference by architectural surgery, which fine-tunes only the parameters of the new batches Lotfollahi et al., 2022. We train a totalVI reference on the first three sites of the CITE-seq data and map the fourth site as if only its RNA had been measured.

cite = ln.Artifact.get(key="multimodal_integration/neurips2021_cite.h5mu").load()

Mosaic integration with MultiVI¶

In mosaic integration, datasets share only some modalities. MultiVI embeds cells with any combination of RNA, chromatin accessibility and proteins, using the modalities that were measured in each cell Ashuach et al., 2023. We integrate the CITE-seq cells of the first site with the Multiome cells of the second site, which share only their RNA.

def fill_missing(adata: ad.AnnData, obs_names: pd.Index) -> ad.AnnData:
    missing = obs_names.difference(adata.obs_names)
    empty = ad.AnnData(
        sp.csr_matrix((len(missing), adata.n_vars)),
        obs=pd.DataFrame(index=missing),
        var=adata.var,
    )
    return ad.concat([adata, empty], merge="first")[obs_names].copy()

MultiVI improves cell type conservation and batch mixing over the PCA of the shared genes, but the low iLISI and kBET scores confirm that the datasets are not fully mixed. Mosaic integrations therefore have to be checked against known cell types or held-out modalities before the joint embedding is used for downstream analyses.

Further methods¶

Seurat’s bridge integration maps unimodal queries onto a reference through a multimodal bridge dataset and was among the best methods for transferring labels from RNA to ATAC data Hao et al., 2024Wang & Zhao, 2026, but it is only available in R. For mosaic data of RNA and proteins, MIDAS and StabMap performed best in benchmarks, and MIDAS imputed missing modalities most accurately He et al., 2024Ghazanfar et al., 2024Fu et al., 2025. As in any integration, the results should be checked against known cell types and the measured modalities, as we did above.
